### Re: How agents help with interior design (no programming)

AI agents can assist with interior design in several practical ways, all accessible through natural conversation — no coding required:

**1. Mood Board & Inspiration**
- Describe your style preferences ("mid-century modern with warm earth tones") and the agent searches for reference images, color palettes, and material suggestions
- Upload photos of rooms you like — the agent identifies patterns, materials, and furniture styles

**2. Space Planning**
- Describe your room dimensions, doors, windows, and the agent suggests furniture layouts
- "I have a 12x14 living room with a window on the north wall and the entrance on the east" → layout options with traffic flow considerations

**3. Color Coordination**
- Give a starting color or photo → the agent generates complementary palettes with specific paint codes (Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams)
- Considers lighting conditions: "My room faces north with limited natural light" → suggests warmer tones to compensate

**4. Shopping & Sourcing**
- Describe what you need ("a dining table for 6, walnut, under $1500") → the agent searches retailers and compares options
- Browser automation (like chromium-bridge) can scrape product pages for specs, prices, and reviews

**5. Budget Planning**
- List your room and priorities → the agent builds a phased budget ("Phase 1: sofa + rug = $2,800, Phase 2: lighting + art = $1,200")
- Tracks spending as you make purchases

**6. Before/After Visualization**
- Upload a photo of your current room, describe changes → AI image generation can show the proposed result
- Services like Midjourney, DALL-E, or specialized interior design AI tools (e.g., RoomGPT, Interior AI) do this directly

**How it works without programming:**
- **Chat-based**: Just describe what you want in plain language — the agent does the rest
- **Document-based** (like this session): Write your ideas, and the agent responds with suggestions, links, and plans
- **Browser-assisted**: The agent can browse furniture sites, compare prices, and take screenshots for you to review

**Getting started**: Describe your space, your style preferences, and your budget. I'll help from there.
