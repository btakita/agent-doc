### Context (transferred from tasks/software/agent-doc.md)

Systems Theory is a large field of study that encompasses the analogy pattern — recognizing structural relationships that transfer across domains. In the agent-doc.md session, we identified that:

- An **analogy** is a **Pattern** recognized across **Domains**: the structural relationship between abstractions in one domain maps to the same structural relationship in another
- Example: NULL:database :: noumena:ontology — the *relationship* (content-free pointer to unknown) is the pattern that transfers
- Patterns expressed at the right level of abstraction (vague enough to transfer, precise enough to be meaningful) become reusable across any domain
- The Existence Lang ontology provides vocabulary for expressing these transferable patterns
