DREAMABLE, INC.
SETTLEMENT AND MUTUAL RELEASE AGREEMENT

This Settlement and Mutual Release (“Agreement”) is entered into as of May 28, 2026, between Dreamable,

Inc. with a primary address at 846A North Point St. San Francisco, CA (“Company”) and Brian Takita

(“Consultant”) with a primary address at PO Box 571 / 1 Park St. North Grosvenordale, CT 06255.

RECITALS

The parties entered into a Consulting Agreement and Addendum, both effective April 5, 2026 (together,

"Consulting Agreement"), pursuant to which Consultant provided software development services through

May 26, 2026. An email was sent on April 6, 2026, terminating the Consulting Agreement; however, the

parties nevertheless continued the engagement under the terms of the Consulting Agreement, except for a

modification to the payment amount. The parties wish to resolve all matters between them, confirm their

respective rights, and provide for a final settlement.

TERMS

1.  Engagement Period. Consultant provided services to Company from April 6, 2026 - May 26, 2026.

2.  Settlement Payment. Company agrees to pay Consultant $5,000 as full and final settlement to resolve

all matters between the parties. Upon payment, all compensation obligations of Company to Consultant

are satisfied in full.

3.

Intellectual Property. Consultant acknowledges and confirms that all inventions, code, documentation,

and work product developed for Company or in connection with the Services during the engagement are

and have been the sole property of Company. Consultant reaffirms his waiver of any rights with respect

to such work. Consultant further acknowledges and agrees that he has no claim of any kind to any other

product, service, concept, or invention of Company, whether or not Consultant was made aware of or

had discussions about such products during the engagement. To the extent the work described in this

Section was not covered by a prior written assignment, Consultant hereby assigns all right, title, and

interest in such work to Company.

4.  Prior Inventions. Consultant retains full and exclusive ownership of all Prior Inventions listed in the

Addendum to the Consulting Agreement. Company has no claim to any Prior Invention. Company
receives only a nonexclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use any Prior Inventions incorporated into

Company's products under their published open-source license terms.

5.  Confidentiality. Consultant agrees to hold in strictest confidence, and not to use or disclose, any

Confidential Information of Company. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information

relating to Company's business, including technical data, trade secrets, product plans, customer

information, finances, operations, and its personnel. This obligation survives termination of this

Agreement.

6.  Company Property. All documents, code, and materials created or received in connection with the

Services are property of Company. Consultant agrees not to use or disclose any such materials following

the conclusion of the engagement.

7.  Mutual Release. The parties fully and finally release each other from any and all claims, demands,

actions, causes of action, obligations, damages, and liabilities of any kind whatsoever, known or

unknown, arising out of or related to the consulting engagement, the services rendered, or any matter
