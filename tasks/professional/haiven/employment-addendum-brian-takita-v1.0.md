1. Equity Grant
Subject to Board of Directors approval, the Company will grant Employee an option to purchase
16,053 shares of the Company’s common stock which is equal to one percent (1.00%) of the
Company’s fully diluted capitalization measured immediately after the grant (the “Option”).

●  The fully diluted denominator will include all outstanding common and preferred shares,
all shares reserved under the equity incentive plan (whether granted or ungranted),
warrants, SAFEs, and convertible notes (on an as-converted basis).

●  The Board will consider and, if approved, authorize the grant within thirty (30) days after

Employee’s start date of July 29, 2026.

●  Vesting will commence on the July 29, 2026 start date regardless of the later date of

Board approval or execution of the option agreement.

●  25% of the Option will vest on the one-year anniversary of the start date; the remaining
75% will vest in equal monthly installments over the following thirty-six (36) months,
subject to continuous service.

●  The Option will be an Incentive Stock Option (ISO) to the maximum extent permitted
under Section 422 of the Internal Revenue Code; any excess will be a Non-Qualified
Stock Option (NSO).

●  Exercise price will equal the fair market value on the grant date as determined by the
most recent 409A valuation (or Board determination if no current valuation exists).
●  Option term and post-termination exercise periods will be as set forth in the Company’s

equity incentive plan and the form of stock option agreement.

●  Change-of-control treatment will be as provided in the equity incentive plan (or as

otherwise determined by the Board in connection with the grant).

The Company will provide Employee with the current equity incentive plan and the proposed
form of stock option agreement prior to or concurrent with execution of this Addendum. The

2. Approved Outside Engagements
The Company acknowledges and approves the continuation of the following pre-existing outside
activities, provided they do not materially interfere with Employee’s duties to the
Company and Employee maintains strict logical and physical separation of confidential
information, accounts, repositories, credentials, and work product:

(a) Apollo Solutions (technology engagement in which Acadian Financial Services is the
current end client; Employee does not contract directly with Acadian);

(b) MonsterRodHolders.com (independently owned business and associated website,
products, software, content, operations, compensation, and intellectual property); and

(c) Equity Funding Source (independent engagement and associated website, software,
content, operations, compensation, and intellectual property).

Notwithstanding Sections 3, 4, 8, and 11 of the Agreement and the Conflict of Interest
Guidelines (Exhibit B):

●  All compensation, fees, equity, work product, inventions, and confidential information
arising from the approved activities remain the sole property of the Employee or the
applicable third party, as required by their governing agreements.

●  The Company acquires no ownership interest or license in any such work product or
intellectual property merely because the activities continue during employment.
●  The Company will not require disclosure of any third party’s confidential information.
●  This approval may not be revoked retroactively and may not be used to claim

compensation already earned under the approved activities.

●  Any future conflict concern will be addressed prospectively and in good faith.

Employee may submit additional outside activities for written approval under the same
conflict-based standard. Any activity approved in writing will receive the same compensation,
intellectual-property, and confidentiality protections set forth above. Section 11 of the Agreement
is modified solely with respect to the activities approved under this Section 2 (and any future
activities approved in writing); it otherwise remains in full force.

4. Corrections to Termination and Related Terms

(a) The final non-competition paragraph in Exhibit C (referencing Section 10, the
“Geographic Area,” and a two-year restriction) is deleted in its entirety. Section 10 remains
intentionally struck.

(b) If the Company terminates Employee’s employment without Cause before the one-year
cliff, Employee will receive actual vesting credit equal to 1/48 of the total Option shares for
each full month of continuous service completed prior to the termination date. “Cause”
means (i) willful failure to perform material duties after written notice and a reasonable
opportunity to cure, (ii) gross negligence or willful misconduct that materially harms the
Company, (iii) conviction of (or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to) a felony or crime
involving moral turpitude, (iv) material breach of the Agreement or this Addendum that
remains uncured after written notice, or (v) fraud, embezzlement, or material dishonesty
against the Company.

(c) Signing-bonus repayment: If Employee voluntarily resigns or is terminated for Cause
within twelve (12) months of the start date, Employee will repay a prorated portion of the
signing bonus actually paid, calculated as (amount paid) × (number of full months remaining
in the 12-month period ÷ 12). No repayment is required if employment ends by termination
without Cause, layoff, death, disability, or resignation for Good Reason (as that term may be
defined in any subsequent agreement). The second $10,000 installment will be paid on or
before December 31, 2026, regardless of whether an “official launch” is declared.
