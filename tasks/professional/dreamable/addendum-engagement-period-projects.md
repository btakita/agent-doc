ADDENDUM — ENGAGEMENT-PERIOD PROJECTS
======================================

*Supplement to Exhibit A of the Addendum to the Dreamable Consulting Agreement (effective April 5, 2026), executed by Brian Takita ("Consultant") in connection with the Dreamable, Inc. Settlement and Mutual Release Agreement dated May 28, 2026.*

Purpose
-------

The original Addendum to the Consulting Agreement included an Exhibit A enumerating Consultant's Prior Inventions as of the April 5, 2026 Effective Date, plus a categorical open-source carve-out at §2(b) covering general-purpose, OSI-licensed software created by Consultant on Consultant's own time without use of Company confidential information.

This document identifies the open-source and personal repositories Consultant created **during** the engagement window (April 6, 2026 – May 26, 2026) that fall under that carve-out. It is provided to remove any ambiguity regarding Section 3 (Intellectual Property) of the Settlement and Mutual Release Agreement, so the parties have a contemporaneous record that these projects are general-purpose Consultant property, not work product developed for Company or in connection with the Services.

Scope
-----

The carve-out applies as follows:

- **Listed projects** were created on Consultant's own time, without use of Company confidential information, and address general-purpose engineering needs unrelated to Company's business.
- **Open-source projects** in the list are published under OSI-approved licenses and are subject to Addendum §2(b).
- Pursuant to Addendum §2(b) and Section 4 of the Settlement Agreement, Company receives a nonexclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use any of these works under their published open-source license terms; no additional rights vest in Company by operation of the Consulting Agreement, the Settlement Agreement, or otherwise.
