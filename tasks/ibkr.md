### Top Things to Be Aware Of

**1. IBKR Does NOT Make Margin Calls — It Liquidates Without Notice**
This is the single most consequential and unusual provision. If your account is under-margined, IBKR reserves the right to close out positions immediately, in any manner, without contacting you first. You cannot choose which positions they liquidate. Don't rely on auto-liquidation as a stop-loss strategy.

**2. You Can Lose More Than You Deposit**
Margin trading, futures, forex, and options (especially uncovered writing) all carry the risk of losses exceeding your initial investment. Naked call writing has *unlimited* loss potential.

**3. No Investment Advice — Strictly Self-Directed**
IBKR employees are prohibited from making recommendations. No suitability assessment is performed. All trading decisions are entirely yours.

**4. Mandatory Arbitration & Class Action Waiver**
Disputes (especially futures/forex/crypto) go to binding arbitration. You waive the right to participate in class actions. The arbitration agreement binds your spouse, heirs, and next-of-kin.

**5. IBKR Has Broad Unilateral Discretion**
They can terminate service, change margin requirements, restrict trading, refuse orders, and liquidate positions — all at any time, without notice.

### Crypto/Digital Assets (Zero Hash)

- All crypto services are provided by **Zero Hash**, not IBKR. IBKR is not liable for any crypto losses, theft, or security breaches.
- **No SIPC, FDIC, or government insurance** on crypto assets.
- Crypto has **zero margin value** — can't offset IB margin requirements.
- No short selling of digital assets. Must be fully funded.
- Zero Hash can suspend/terminate your account at any time without notice and liquidate remaining assets.
- Zero Hash's total liability is capped at the lesser of 12 months of fees paid or **$100**.
- Blockchain transfers are irreversible.

### Options (from the ODD + OCC disclosures)

- Options can expire worthless (100% loss of premium). Time decay works against holders.
- Uncovered call writers face unlimited loss. Assignment can happen at any time (American-style), including after hours.
- Index options with AM settlement can diverge significantly from the prior close.
- OCC auto-exercises options in-the-money by $0.01+ at expiration unless you instruct otherwise.
- If you lack equity at expiration, IBKR may decline to exercise or may exercise and immediately liquidate.

---

### Futures & Forex

- Small margin relative to contract value = leveraged risk. A small move can wipe out your margin.
- Forex: **IBKR is your counterparty** — direct conflict of interest. Your deposits have no regulatory protection under NFA/CFTC rules.
- ~44-47% of retail forex accounts were profitable in 2025; ~53-56% were unprofitable.
- Stop-loss orders may not work in illiquid or gapped markets.

### Fees & Conflicts

- **Commissions:** Tiered, fixed, or zero-commission (IBKR-LITE) plans.
- **IBKR-LITE:** Zero commission but orders routed to market makers who pay IBKR for order flow — may reduce price improvement.
- **IBKR-PRO:** SmartRouting for best execution; IBKR may receive exchange rebates.
- IBKR may **internalize** whole-share order flow through an affiliate (you can opt out for whole shares but NOT fractional shares).
- Margin interest, exchange/regulatory fees, market data subscriptions, and corporate action fees apply separately.
- IBKR receives 12b-1 fees from mutual fund companies.

---

### Securities Lending Program

- You can lend fully-paid securities; IB provides collateral (cash/Treasuries) at 100% market value.
- **Loaned securities may NOT be protected by SIPC.**
- You lose voting rights on lent shares. Substitute dividends taxed at ordinary income rates (not qualified dividend rates).
- IB can terminate loans at any time and is not obligated to lend your securities or get you the best rates.

### Other Notable Items

- **Fractional shares** are not transferable to another broker — must be liquidated. No voting rights.
- **Leveraged/inverse ETFs** reset daily — long-term performance deviates from the stated multiple. Not suitable for buy-and-hold.
- **After-hours trading:** Lower liquidity, wider spreads, exaggerated price effects from news.
- **Business continuity:** IBKR aims to restore access within 24 hours of disruption (not guaranteed). You must maintain backup trading arrangements.
- **Privacy:** IBKR collects extensive personal data (including biometrics) and shares with affiliates globally, cloud providers, exchanges, and law enforcement.
- **IBKR has disciplinary history** — details at Investor.gov/CRS.

### Bottom Line

The agreements are heavily weighted toward protecting IBKR. The three biggest practical risks are: (1) automatic liquidation without margin calls, (2) unlimited loss potential on uncovered options/short futures, and (3) no government insurance on crypto assets. If you're primarily trading stocks with a cash or standard margin account, most of the exotic risk disclosures (forex counterparty risk, crypto Zero Hash terms) won't apply day-to-day, but the margin liquidation policy and the broad discretionary powers are always in effect.
