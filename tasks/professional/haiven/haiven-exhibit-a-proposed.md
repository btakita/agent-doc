## EFFECT OF LISTING

1. Employee retains full and exclusive ownership of Employee's rights in the
   listed Prior Inventions. Haiven has no ownership claim to a listed Prior
   Invention.
2. The listed Prior Inventions include all versions existing before the
   Employment Commencement Date and Employee's rights in their modifications,
   enhancements, updates, ports, bindings, derivative works, documentation,
   and successor projects. Continued maintenance or development during
   employment does not by itself transfer ownership to Haiven.
3. Listing, disclosure, evaluation, discussion, or permitted continuation of
   a Prior Invention does not itself grant Haiven an ownership interest or
   license.
4. If Employee incorporates a Prior Invention into a Haiven product, process,
   or machine with Haiven's prior written authorization, Haiven receives only
   the rights available under the Prior Invention's then-applicable published
   open-source license or under a separate written license signed by Employee
   and Haiven. No broader implied or automatic license arises from
   incorporation.
5. Nothing in this Exhibit A requires Employee to disclose source code,
   credentials, trade secrets, client information, or other material that
   Employee is not authorized to disclose.
