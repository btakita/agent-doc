Key Talking Points

  • AI grounded in truth — verifiable outputs + citations prevent malpractice risk from hallucination
  • System of record first — AI is only as good as the data it touches; Filevine owns the data layer
  • Compliance as a feature — FedRAMP/HIPAA/SOC 2 are product differentiators for gov/enterprise
  • Legal ≠ generic SaaS — jurisdictional deadlines, privileged docs, regulated billing = real domain complexity
  • Interactive artifacts gap — LOIS generates static outputs; making artifacts conversational is the next frontier
  • Ontology for claim-level grounding — citation-level verification (current) vs. formal evidence graph (next)
