Subcontractor Agreement

This agreement (“Agreement”) is made effective as of 04 / 29 / 2026 (the “Commencement Date”)
between Test Double Inc., an Ohio corporation (the “Company”), and Brian Kenji Takita (the
“Subcontractor” and together, the “Parties”), who hereby agree as follows:

1.  Engagement.  Upon the terms and subject to the conditions described in this Agreement, the
Company hereby engages the Subcontractor, and the Subcontractor hereby accepts such

engagement with the Company, to perform the activities  indicated on the Statement of Work
(the “Services”) attached hereto as Exhibit A. The Subcontractor shall devote to the Company
such portion of their professional time, attention, energy, loyalty, and skill as may be necessary
to fully perform the Services and hereby promises to perform and discharge the Services.  Work

that the Subcontractor performs for the Company may be performed for the benefit of third-
party clients of the Company and such third-party clients are hereinafter referred to as a
“Client” or “Clients”.

2.  Compensation.

1.  Payment.  In exchange for the satisfactory performance of the Services the Company
shall pay the Subcontractor a weekly rate in the amount set forth on the Statement of
Work (the “Compensation”). Subcontractor shall submit to Company semi-monthly
invoices for the number of days worked for the prior invoicing period (1st to the 15th or
16th to the last day of the month). All invoices must be delivered within ten (10) days of

the completion of the invoiced work. Payment to Subcontractor shall be made within 30

days of the receipt of each invoice from the Subcontractor.

2.  Breach.  In the event that Subcontractor fails to terminate this Agreement pursuant to

Section 4 or breaches any other provision(s) in and of this Agreement, Subcontractor will
have ten (10) days from the receipt of written notice thereof to cure any breach or default

hereunder; thereafter, if any breach or default remains uncured, Subcontractor shall
forfeit any rights to any pending or future payments from Company.  Payment to the
Subcontractor is specifically agreed not to constitute or imply acceptance by Company of
any portion of the Subcontractorʼs work.

3.  Covenants.

1.  Confidentiality.  Subcontractor shall forever treat as confidential and not in any manner
use, disseminate, publish, or disclose, except as is necessary in the ordinary course of

business and Subcontractor is authorized, any information regarding the Companyʼs or
Clientʼs business including, without limitation, and whether or not marked as
“confidential” and in whatever form or medium, internal business information, trade
secrets, proprietary data, financial information, intellectual property, customers lists,

supplier lists, original works of authorship, know-how or other intangible property
protectable under federal, state or foreign law, which shall have come or shall hereafter
come into Subcontractorʼs possession or which Subcontractor learns.

2.  Non-solicitation.  From the Commencement Date until 12 months after the termination

of this Agreement (the “Restricted Period”), Subcontractor will not, without the
Companyʼs or Clientʼs prior written consent, directly or indirectly,

1.  perform for the Client services of any kind or enter into any Agreement to perform
such services either as an employee or independent contractor for the Client that
would bypass the role Company is playing in the overall transaction;

2.  solicit or encourage any employee or contractor of the Company, a Client, or

affiliates of either to terminate employment with, or cease providing services to,
the Company, a Client, or affiliates of either; or

with the Companyʼs relationship with any person who is or during the period of

Subcontractorʼs engagement by the Company was a contractor, partner, supplier,
customer or client of the Company, a Client, or affiliates of either.

3.  Acknowledgment.  The Subcontractor acknowledges that (i) the foregoing covenants are
fundamental and essential for protecting the Companyʼs legitimate business interests,

including without limitation interests in the Companyʼs confidential information, and not
to arbitrarily restrict or prevent the Subcontractor from utilizing their general technical

skills, (ii) such provisions are reasonable and appropriate in all respects, and (iii) in the
event of any violation by the Subcontractor of any of such covenants, the Company

would suffer irreparable harm and its remedies at law would be inadequate.  In the event
of any violation or attempted violation of such covenants by the Subcontractor, the
Company shall be entitled to a temporary restraining order, temporary and permanent
injunctions, specific performance and other equitable relief without any showing of

irreparable harm or damage or the posting of any bond, in addition to any other rights or
remedies which may then be available to the Company.

4.  Term and Termination.

1.  Term.  The term of Subcontractor's engagement shall begin as of the Commencement

Date and shall continue until the earlier of (i) the termination date indicated on the
Statement of Work, (ii) the completion of Services, or (iii) early termination by either
party.

2.  Termination by Subcontractor.  If the Subcontractor terminates the agreement within the
first 60 days of the start date,  they forfeit any outstanding invoices. Post the first 60 days,
in the event that Subcontractor wishes to terminate this Agreement prior to the end date
or there is no reasonably clear ending date in the Statement of Work, Subcontractor may

terminate engagement by sending written notice of such termination to the Company at
least 30 days before ending work. In the event of such termination, Company shall pay
the Subcontractor for the Services performed satisfactorily prior to termination within 30
days of receipt of an invoice provided Subcontractor has not materially breached this

Agreement.

3.  Termination by Company.  Company may terminate this Agreement, in whole or in part,
by written notice to the Subcontractor. In the event of such termination, Company shall
pay the Subcontractor for the Services performed satisfactorily prior to termination
within 30 days of receipt of an invoice thereof.

5.  Work For Hire.  The Subcontractor expressly acknowledges and agrees that any work prepared

by the Subcontractor under this Agreement shall be considered “work for hire” and the
exclusive property of the Company or Client unless otherwise specified. To the extent such

work may not be deemed a “work for hire” under applicable law, the Subcontractor hereby
assigns to the Company or Client all of its right, title, and interest in and to such work. The
Subcontractor shall execute and deliver to the Company any instruments of transfer and take

such other action that the Company may reasonably request, including, without limitation,
executing and filing, at the Companyʼs expense, copyright applications, assignments, and other

documents required for the protection of the Companyʼs rights to such materials.
Insurance.  Subcontractor will maintain insurance as required for specific engagements and

provide evidence from time to time to Company that Subcontractor meets such requirements.

2.  Benefits. The Company and Subcontractor agree that Subcontractor will receive no

8.  Compliance.  The Subcontractor agrees to be bound by and, at its own cost, to comply with all
federal, state and local laws, ordinances and regulations applicable to the Subcontractorʼs
Services. The Subcontractor shall be liable to Company for all liabilities, losses, costs and
expenses including, but not limited to, any failure of the Subcontractor or its agents, employees

or sub-contractors to comply with laws, as imposed upon Company or Clients as a result

thereof.
9.  Warranty.

1.  Third-Party Rights.  Subcontractor warrants that no work performed or intellectual

property utilized in the performance of Services violates the rights of any third-party. In
the event that the Services provided by the Subcontractor violate the rights of any third-

party, Subcontractor agrees to indemnify Company for all damages, losses, expenses,
costs, and attorneysʼ fees whatsoever resulting from the breach of this Agreement by the

Recipient.

2.  Quality of Service.  Every part of the Services shall be executed in strict accordance with

this Agreement and in a sound, workmanlike, efficient and substantial manner. The
Subcontractor agrees to use reasonable care, skill, competence and judgment in the

performance of its Services hereunder.

10.  Other Requirements.

1.  Subcontracting.  The Subcontractor may not use further subcontractors or

subconsultants to perform any of the Services without prior written consent of the
Company.

2.  References to Work Performed.  Subcontractor shall not reference the Client nor work

completed for Company or Client in promotional materials or in any manner to promote
the services of the Subcontractor.

11.  Indemnification.  The Subcontractor agrees to indemnify, defend and hold harmless Company
and Clients and their present and future officers, directors, employees, owners, agents, sub-

contractors, sub-consultants, successors and assigns against all claims and liabilities including
consequential or indirect damages arising out of, related to, or based upon the negligence or
willful misconduct of the Subcontractor or a breach of this Agreement by Subcontractor.

12.  Auditing.  The Subcontractor shall keep a detailed record of its costs associated with Services.

Company or Clients may inspect these records during regular office hours, on receipt of
reasonable notice, for a period of two years following the completion of the Services.

13.  Attorneysʼ Fees.  If there is any litigation or other action taken by any party or parties to enforce

or interpret any provisions of or rights arising under this Agreement, the non-prevailing party or
parties shall pay to the prevailing party or parties all costs and expenses, including but not
limited to reasonable attorneysʼ fees and costs, which the prevailing party or parties incur in
enforcing this Agreement or in pursuing any remedy allowed by law, whether such is incurred

by the filing of suit or otherwise, in preparation for or at trial, or in any appeal.

14.  Miscellaneous.  This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
the State of Ohio.  The parties designate the courts located in Columbus, Ohio as courts of
proper jurisdiction and venue with respect to any action or controversy under the Agreement
and waive any defenses to jurisdiction and venue with respect to any actions brought in such

courts.  This Agreement is binding upon and inures to the benefit of and is enforceable by the

parties to this Agreement and their respective successors and assigns.  This Agreement
constitutes the entire agreement of the parties and supersedes any prior agreements and

understandings, whether oral or written, between them with respect to the subject matter of
this Agreement.  The headings of this Agreement are for convenience only and will not control
or affect the meaning or construction of the provisions of this Agreement.  This Agreement may

not be terminated, modified, or amended orally or by any course of conduct or usage of trade
except by an agreement in writing duly executed by the parties.  Any waiver of a breach of any

provisions of this Agreement is limited to the particular breach, and should not be deemed to
be a continuing waiver of the same breach, and should not be deemed a waiver of any other

provision of this Agreement.  If any portion of this Agreement is determined to be
unenforceable or invalid, the remainder of this Agreement remains binding and effective

against all parties and their respective successors and assigns.  This Agreement may be
executed in multiple counterparts, each of which will be deemed an original, but all of which

taken together shall constitute one and the same Agreement.  All representations, warranties,
covenants and agreements in or under this Agreement, and in any other documents executed
or delivered pursuant to this Agreement or in connection with the transactions contemplated
by this Agreement, survive the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement.  Each

party will execute, acknowledge or verify, and deliver any and all documents which may from
time to time be reasonably requested by the other party to carry out the purpose and intent of
this Agreement.
