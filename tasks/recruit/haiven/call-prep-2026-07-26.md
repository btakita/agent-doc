# Call Prep — Brian + Luke + Craig, tonight 8pm ET (2026-07-26)

Purpose: comp alignment call. Craig's email signals: they want you ("mutually agreeable
path"), the offer will be a "blend" of money + mission + merit + "significant upside,"
and the team INCLUDING Craig is at ~50% of market "until revenue and funding allow."
Expect a below-ask cash number wrapped in upside. This brief is the playbook.

## Before the call (do these first)

1. **Compute your real family cashflow floor.** Your $180k negotiation floor was set
   against Acadian; it is NOT your survival number. Decide the minimum base that
   genuinely works for your family for ~12 months (call it $F). Everything between $F
   and $185k is trade-space you can convert into equity + triggers. Do not enter the
   call without knowing $F.
2. Re-read the two tells: written Founder's Package definition; engagement on cliff
   protection. Tonight is where these get answered live.

## The key reframe — your fairness instinct is right, your remedy is backwards

Not wanting to be the only full-salary person on a 50% team is honorable AND correct
(new-hire-paid-most is corrosive). But the fair structure is NOT "give up equity."
Startup-standard fairness is the opposite trade:

> **Team-parity-ish cash NOW + MORE equity + a WRITTEN salary-normalization trigger.**

- Below-market cash is a loan you make the company; equity + triggers are the interest.
  Giving up cash AND equity is just donating.
- The trigger must be contractual, not vibes: "base adjusts to $___k upon [seed close /
  $___ revenue / Series A], retroactive-or-not stated." Craig's "once revenue and
  funding allow" is a promise; make it a term.
- Optional additions: salary accrual (deferred comp paid at trigger), or milestone
  bonuses tied to the same August-beta/October-launch gates their funding rides on.
- If they discount cash by ~X% for parity, equity should move meaningfully above 1.0%
  (e.g., 1.5-2.0%+) — at 50% of market they are all being paid in upside; you should
  be too.

## Call sequencing (order matters)

1. **Let them present the blend FIRST.** Craig has one prepared ("resulting offer will
   be a blend"). Do not bid against yourself before hearing it.
2. **Ask the definition questions live**: Founder's Package instrument, fully-diluted
   basis, pool size, vesting, any Founder's-Pack revenue link; team size + who is at
   what discount; months of runway at current burn; WHAT trigger normalizes salaries
   and to what number.
3. **State the family constraint plainly, without apology** (they are financially
   better-positioned than you; that's a constraint statement, not a grievance):
   "I have a family cashflow reality that sets a floor on near-term cash. Above that
   floor, I'm genuinely flexible and happy to be paid in the same upside you are."
4. **If the blend clears $F**: negotiate the trigger + equity bump + protections
   (clawback scoping, pre-cliff vesting credit) into it. Use the saved line to dignify
   any concession: "the work and what I'd learn here matter to me... that's why I can
   meet you at ___."
5. **Only if the blend cannot clear $F**: introduce the Acadian+prototype path — as a
   bridge, not an exit: "Then here's how I can still help while the finances mature:
   I take the contract seat for family stability and build you the SDK prototype on a
   bounded basis... and when the salary adjustment lands, we revisit this conversation
   with the prototype already shipped." (Materials ready: decline-draft.md one-para
   swap + sdk-contract-structure.md.) Do NOT open with this; it invites them to take
   the cheap option before trying to close you.
6. **Do not accept a number live.** Close with: "Let me run this against my family
   budget tonight; you'll have my answer tomorrow." 24 hours is normal; live-accepting
   a discounted package is how regret gets signed.

## Red lines (unchanged)

- No package without the Founder's Package in writing.
- IP carve-out (pre-existing + future side work) stays in the offer letter.
- Clawback scoped to voluntary/cause; pre-cliff vesting credit if terminated without
  cause. These matter MORE at 50% salary, not less: you'd be underpaid AND unvested.
