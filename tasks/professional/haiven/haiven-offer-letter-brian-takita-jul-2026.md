Dear Brian:

On behalf of everyone at Haiven, Inc (“Company” and “Haiven”), it is my privilege and pleasure
to offer you the position of Backend Software Engineer, subject to the details below. This is a
full-time remote position, with an expected workload of at least 40 hours per week.

This  offer  must  be  accepted  by  6pm  PT,  Tuesday  July  28,  2026,  or  it  will  be  withdrawn.
Assuming  acceptance,  you  will  start  on  or  before  Wednesday,  July  29,  2026,  at  9am  PT  for
initial onboarding. You will report to Eddie Joseph, VP of Product, located in Spokane, WA.

Compensation. Your annual salary rate will be USD $155,000, paid twice monthly on the 15th
and  31st.  Payment  of  your  salary  shall  be  made  less  all  legally  applicable  deductions  and  in
accordance with Haiven’s regular payroll schedule for employees of a similar role and position.
As an option to you, the Company can pay part of this salary as a pre-tax offset for your home
office  rent,  calculated  as  a  percentage  of  your  home  used for work times monthly rent. As an
example: if your office is 20% of your total square footage, then 20% x monthly rent in pre-tax,
paid directly to your landlord’s bank account. There is tax precedent for this compensation.

Signing bonus. You will be eligible for a one-time signing bonus of $20,000. This bonus will be
paid  in  two  equal  installments:  $10,000  will be paid immediately upon your start date, and the
remaining  $10,000  will be paid at the platform’s official launch, which is scheduled for October
2026, but no later than December 2026. In the spirit of fairness, should you voluntarily resign or
be  terminated  for  cause  within  12  months  of  your  start  date,  you  agree  to  repay  a  prorated
portion of this signing bonus. Furthermore, if you are terminated by the Company without cause
before the 1-year equity cliff, you will receive prorated vesting credit for your equity options.

Equity.  As  part  of  this  offer,  you  are  eligible  to  receive  one  percent  (1.0%)  Company  stock
options.  25%  of  the  options  shall  vest on the one-year anniversary of your start date, with the
remaining  75%  vesting  in  equal  monthly  installments  over  the following 36 months. Additional
details and paperwork will be discussed as part of your onboarding process.

Benefits.  This  offer  includes  two  (2)  weeks  of  paid  vacation,  plus  11  paid  holidays.  Your
employment  also  includes  medical,  dental,  vision  insurance,  and  any  other  future  company
provided  benefits. Details on enrolling family members for insurance coverage will be provided
during  your  onboarding  process.  Other  benefits  may  also  be  available to you over time. If so,
you will be provided with additional information that outlines the details of these benefits.

At Will Status. It is our hope and expectation that you will be a long-term employee.  However,
like  all  employees  of  the  Company  you  have  the  right  to  resign,  with  or  without  cause.  The
Company  likewise  reserves  the  right  to  modify  or  terminate  your  employment  with  or  without
cause. Only the CEO and COO of the Company have the authority to enter into any agreement
guaranteeing  you  employment  for  any  specified  period  of  time  or  modifying  the  nature  of  the
at-will  relationship between you and the Company. No other employee or representative of the
Company  has  the  authority  to  do  so.  If  you  choose  to  terminate  your  employment,  you  are
requested to provide a minimum of 15 working days’ notice, in writing.

Conditions  of  Employment.  As  a  condition  of  employment  you  will  be  required  to  sign  and
return a Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement. The Company will also
conduct reference checks and a third-party background check to verify no prior criminal record.

Prior  Intellectual  Property.  Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement or the
Confidentiality  and  Intellectual  Property  Assignment  Agreement,  you  retain  all  right,  title,  and
interest in and to your pre-existing open-source projects and independent side work, which are
excluded from the definition of Work Product and Company Inventions, provided that such work
was  developed  entirely  on  your  own  time  without  using  any  Company  equipment,  supplies,
facilities,  or  Confidential Information and does not relate directly to the Company's business or
actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development.

Honoring Prior Obligations. This offer is based upon the Company’s understanding that your
acceptance of this position will not breach any agreement you have entered into with any other
person  or  entity,  including,  without  limitation,  any  agreement  regarding  non-competition,
non-solicitation,  or  confidentiality.  You  agree  that  you  shall  not  (i)  bring  any  confidential  or
proprietary  information  that  you  may  have  received  from  any  other  person  or  entity, including
prior or current employment or consultancies, into the Company; (ii) give any such information to
any Company employee or consultant; or (iii) use or permit use of any such information.

External  Contracting. Limited external contracting is permissible, provided that such activities
do not interfere with your responsibilities at Haiven and are disclosed to the Company.

Entire Agreement. This offer letter, when signed by you, constitutes a binding agreement, and
together  with  the  Confidentiality  and  Intellectual  Property  Assignment  Agreement  and  Stock
Option  Agreement,  constitutes  the  entire  agreement  between  the  parties  with  respect  to  the
matters  discussed  herein  and  supersedes  any  other  agreement  or  understanding  (oral  or
written) which may exist between the parties.

Miscellaneous.  Your  employment  will  be  subject  to  the provisions of an Employee Handbook
outlining various policies of Haiven, and an Intellectual Property Agreement, both of which will,
along  with  this  letter,  govern  your  relationship  with  Haiven.  You will be required to review and
sign all of these documents as a condition of employment.

This offer letter and all related matters shall be governed by Wyoming law. This offer letter may
be  assigned  by  the  Company  but  is  not  otherwise  assignable.  Any  action  or  proceeding  in
connection  with  this  offer  letter  shall  be  brought  in  Laramie  County,  Wyoming.  You  agree  to
submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in that county.
