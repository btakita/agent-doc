### Re: Recovering brooke-brodack's autodeleted YouTube channel (HEAD)

The options depend on **why** the channel was autodeleted and how much time has passed.

#### Official YouTube recovery (within 30 days)

If the channel was terminated by YouTube (policy violation, spam flag, bulk reporting, etc.):

1. **Appeal the termination** — go to [YouTube's account termination appeal form](https://support.google.com/youtube/contact/channel_appeal). YouTube sometimes reverses bulk-report takedowns, especially for established creators.
2. **Google account recovery** — if the underlying Google account was deleted, try [accounts.google.com/signin/recovery](https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery). Within 30 days, account data may still be restorable.
3. **YouTube Support** — contact directly via [support.google.com/youtube](https://support.google.com/youtube). Escalate to a human reviewer if the automated appeal fails.

After 30 days from account deletion, official recovery is not possible — content is purged from Google's servers.

#### Recovering the video files externally

Even if the channel is gone from YouTube, videos may survive in:

| Source | Where to look |
|--------|--------------|
| **Internet Archive** | [archive.org](https://archive.org) — search "brookebrodack" or paste old video URLs into the Wayback Machine |
| **Lost Media Wiki** | [forums.lostmediawiki.com](https://forums.lostmediawiki.com/thread/5112/brookers-videos-old-youtube-general) — community actively tracks and recovers deleted YouTube content |
| **Google Takeout** — if Brooke has access | If the Google account is still accessible (even without YouTube), [takeout.google.com](https://takeout.google.com) may have the original video files |
| **Third-party downloaders** — cached copies | Sites like Archivarix, Wayback CDX API, or community-shared rips |

#### Background

"Brookers" (Brooke Brodack) has a history with this — her original channel (first most-subscribed on YouTube, 2006) was deleted in 2017. A new channel `@BrookeABrodack` went up in 2018 with ~170 videos and ~2k subscribers as of late 2025.

#### Recommended next steps

1. **File an appeal immediately** if within 30 days of the termination notice
2. **Check Google Takeout** — see if any video data is exportable from the associated Google account
3. **Search archive.org** for the channel name/video URLs
4. **Post in Lost Media Wiki forums** if specific videos are missing — community members often have local copies
