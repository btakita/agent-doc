Brian wants all professional communications (not just specific contacts) to apply bias-stripped prioritization:

## Universal communication principles

1. **Proactive communication** — Reply to every logistical message same-day. Even a brief "let's wait until we talk" counts. Don't let a response to an emotional message stand in for confirming logistics.

2. **Bias-strip before prioritizing:**
   - **Temporal bias**: Recent messages aren't inherently more important than earlier ones
   - **Content length bias**: Long messages aren't inherently more important than short ones
   - Extract the true signal regardless of when it arrived or how much space it took

3. **Capture true priorities explicitly** — When reviewing any professional correspondence, surface what's actually important vs. what's just noise or resolved context.

4. **Logistics must be confirmed explicitly** — "Sounds good" to emotional content does NOT confirm logistical details. Confirm specifics directly.

## How to apply

- When reviewing any contact's messages, apply these principles automatically
- When drafting correspondence, ensure it addresses the true priorities, not just the most recent or longest thread
- Store contact-specific priorities in `mail/contacts/<name>/AGENTS.md` under a "Communication Priorities" section
- Flag when a message pattern shows temporal or length bias obscuring what matters
