WHITEPAPER  |  2025The mid-market paradox

Mid-market leaders feel stuck. On one hand, they are attuned to

market shifts and recognize that AI is imperative for survival.

However, they’re facing a significant hurdle in the actual

execution and integration of AI into their core operations:

• Resource Constraints: Unlike the S&P 500, mid-market firms
rarely have multi-billion-dollar R&D budgets or dedicated AI

research labs. The perceived cost and complexity of custom

AI development can be prohibitive.

• Talent Scarcity: Attracting and retaining top-tier AI engineers,
data scientists, and machine learning specialists is a fierce

competition, often lost to larger enterprises.

• Data Silos and Legacy Systems: Many mid-market companies
have fragmented data across disparate systems (CRM, ERP,

marketing automation, finance, operations), making it

challenging to build the unified data foundation necessary for

effective AI.

can be difficult, resulting in scattered pilot projects rather

than cohesive transformation.

This paradox creates a frustrating loop: leaders know they need

AI to grow, but they lack the internal capacity and resources to

truly implement it effectively.
