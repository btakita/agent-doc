### Re: Finding your address in PNC Mobile

To find the address associated with your PNC account in the mobile app:

1. **Open PNC Mobile** and log in
2. Tap the **profile icon** (or your initials) — usually in the top-left or top-right corner
3. Go to **Settings** or **Profile & Settings**
4. Look for **Personal Information** or **Contact Information**
5. Your mailing address on file should be listed there

If you don't see it under profile settings, try:
- **More** (bottom nav) → **Customer Service** → **Update Contact Information**
- Or search "address" in the app's search/help bar

Note: PNC may only show a partial/masked address for security. To see or update the full address, you might need to call PNC customer service (1-888-762-2265) or visit a branch.
