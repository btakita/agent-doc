## Key Talking Points to Prepare

**1. Their expansion story**
They went from imaging AI → full workflow platform. Voice Notes (ambient scribe) + AutoVerify (RCM) show they're not just selling X-ray analysis — they're selling time and revenue back to practices. Know this arc.

**2. The Aspen Dental rollout**
6-week nationwide rollout is a marquee operational win. Shows enterprise readiness. Good to reference as evidence of mature onboarding and CS infrastructure.

**3. Their moat: 35 FDA clearances**
Regulatory clearances take years. Competitors have far fewer. This is a real barrier, not a marketing claim. Shows clinical rigor and risk management culture.

**4. Canada expansion**
Post-Series B priority. DentalCorp (Canadian) is already a customer. If your role touches growth or partnerships, Canada is live.

**5. Florian Hillen's background**
MIT CS/ML + medical training + McKinsey. The company culture reflects this: they care about clinical outcomes, workflow economics, AND regulatory legitimacy simultaneously. Not a move-fast-break-things shop.

**6. "AI augments, not replaces"**
The company consistently frames AI as supporting clinicians, not replacing them. Adopt this language. The 95%+ user adoption rate is the proof point.
