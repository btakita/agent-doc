---
name: Writing voice — avoid AI tells
description: Use ellipsis (...) instead of em dashes ( — ) in drafted content. Em dash with spaces is a common AI writing tell.
type: feedback
---

Use `...` instead of ` — ` (em dash with spaces) in drafted emails, messages, and prose content. The spaced em dash pattern is a well-known indicator that content was AI-generated.

**Why:** User livestreams and communicates professionally. AI-generated content should not be detectable by style markers. This applies to all drafted correspondence.

**How to apply:** When drafting emails, LinkedIn messages, or any text the user will send as their own, avoid ` — ` constructions. Use `...`, shorter sentences, or restructure to avoid the pattern entirely. Also watch for other common AI tells: "I'd love to", "delve", "leverage", excessive hedging.
