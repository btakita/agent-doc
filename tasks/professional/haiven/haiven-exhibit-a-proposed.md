## Haiven-specific pre-employment materials requiring confirmation

Any Haiven-specific code or other material created before the employment
commencement date, including the repository currently named
`haiven-websocket-hub`, should be classified expressly in the final documents.
This schedule does not assert Employee ownership of material already owned by
Haiven or another party, and storage in an Employee-controlled account does
not by itself determine ownership. Conversely, no Employee-owned
pre-employment material becomes Haiven property merely because it was
discussed with, evaluated for, or later used by Haiven. The parties should
identify the applicable owner and any license in writing before signing.

## Effect of listing

1. The Prior Works remain excluded from "Company Inventions," "Work
   Product," and any other assignment to Haiven. Haiven has no claim to a
   listed Prior Work.
2. Continued maintenance, modification, enhancement, release, or derivative
   development of a listed Prior Work does not by itself transfer ownership to
   Haiven.
3. Haiven receives no ownership or license merely because a Prior Work is
   listed, disclosed, or continued during employment.
4. A Prior Work may be incorporated into a Haiven product only with prior
   written authorization. Haiven receives only the rights available under the
   applicable published open-source license or a separate written license
   signed by Employee and Haiven; incorporation does not create a broader
   implied or automatic license.
5. Nothing in this schedule requires Employee to disclose source code,
   credentials, trade secrets, client information, or other material that
   Employee is not authorized to disclose.

## Proposed accompanying clause for future outside work

The following should appear in the final agreement or a countersigned
addendum, rather than being treated as a Prior Work that already exists:

> **Future Outside Activities.** Employee may undertake future consulting,
> advisory, business, investment, educational, creative, research,
> open-source, and other independent activities. Employee will disclose a new
> paid engagement in writing before beginning it when reasonably practicable.
> General-purpose open-source work, personal research or creative work, and
> passive investments do not require separate approval unless they present an
> actual conflict with Employee's Haiven duties. An outside activity is
> permitted if it does not materially interfere with Employee's duties to
> Haiven, use or disclose Haiven Confidential Information, or constitute work
> assigned to Employee by Haiven. The Company may object to a disclosed paid
> engagement only on reasonable, specific conflict, confidentiality, or
> material-interference grounds stated in writing within ten business days
> after disclosure; otherwise, the engagement is deemed approved. Unanimous
> Board approval is not required. Approval will not be unreasonably withheld,
> conditioned, or delayed and will not be revoked retroactively. All
> compensation, equity, work product, inventions, intellectual property, and
> confidential information arising from a permitted outside activity remain
> with Employee or the applicable third party, as their governing agreements
> require, and Haiven acquires no ownership or license merely because the
> activity occurs during Employee's employment. Any later conflict concern
> will be addressed prospectively and in good faith. Employee will not be
> required to disclose another party's confidential information to obtain or
> retain approval.
